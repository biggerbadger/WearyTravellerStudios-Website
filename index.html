The Wanderer VCO

A precision voltage-controlled oscillator designed for those who seek clarity in the darkness. The Wanderer delivers rich, analog warmth with exceptional tracking stability across the entire frequency spectrum.

Specifications

  • Analog triangle core with temperature compensation
  • Simultaneous sine, triangle, sawtooth, and pulse outputs
  • 1V/octave tracking over 8+ octaves
  • PWM with CV control
  • Linear FM input
  • Hard and soft sync
  • 12HP, 35mm depth
  • +12V: 80mA, -12V: 70mA
